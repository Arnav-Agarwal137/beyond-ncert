1st & 3rd Sundays · ~11 AM / 5 PMFREE · walk-inMumbai

Beyond attending — apply to these. Since you want to do research, look at the Indian Academy of Sciences Summer Research Fellowship (SRFP) and the summer / short-term programmes at CCMB, NCBS, IISc and the IISERs. Most target science undergrads, so check whether MBBS students are eligible — but even one such stint is worth more than a dozen talks.
What to skip, everywhere. Ignore "conference-alert" listing sites pushing a generic "International Conference on Bioinformatics / Physics / AI" that rotates cities monthly — many are predatory, pay-to-publish, and worthless on a CV. Stick to events hosted directly by institutions (IISc, TIFR, NCBS, CCMB, IUCAA, IITs, IISERs) or known communities. And skip pure business-networking mixers.
